About the Application

Drive Zip Extractor is a lightweight cloud utility designed to handle compressed files stored in Google Drive. Rather than requiring users to download multi-gigabyte archives to local hardware, unpack them, and re-upload the files, Drive Zip Extractor processes decompression tasks directly within your Google Drive storage.

Key Functionality

  • Cloud Archive Extraction: Unpack .zip files located in your Google Drive directly into a destination folder of your choice.
  • Selective Inspection: View the contents and directory structure of compressed archives before extracting them.
  • Bandwidth Efficient: Eliminate redundant data transfers across your local network and storage devices.

Google User Data Transparency & Access

Drive Zip Extractor adheres strictly to the principle of least privilege and data minimization:

OAuth Scope Requested: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file

Access Boundaries: We only access the specific archive files you choose to open with the app and create folders for the extracted items. Drive Zip Extractor does not read, scan, index, or modify any other files in your Google Drive.

  • Files are decompressed in temporary runtime memory and are not stored on third-party servers.
  • No humans read, inspect, or analyze file contents.
  • Google Drive user data is never shared, sold, transferred, or used for advertising or AI model training.

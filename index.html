Drive Zip Extractor

Extract and decompress ZIP archives directly in Google Drive without heavy local downloads.

About the Application

Drive Zip Extractor is a lightweight cloud utility designed to handle compressed files stored in Google Drive. Rather than requiring users to download gigabyte-sized archive files to their local devices, extract them, and re-upload the unpacked contents, Drive Zip Extractor handles decompression tasks directly with your Drive storage.

Key Functionality

  • Cloud Archive Extraction: Unpack .zip files located in your Google Drive directly into a folder of your choice.
  • Selective Inspection: View the contents and file lists of compressed archives before choosing to extract them.
  • Bandwidth Efficient: Eliminate the need to route massive file downloads and re-uploads through your local network.

Google User Data Transparency & Access

Drive Zip Extractor values user privacy and adheres to the principle of least privilege:

Scope Used: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file

Purpose: We only access the specific archive files you choose to open with the app and create folders for the extracted items. Drive Zip Extractor does not read, scan, index, or modify any other files in your Google Drive.

  • Files are processed in memory during extraction and are not permanently saved on external servers.
  • Your Google Drive data is never shared, sold, or used for advertising.

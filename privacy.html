Privacy Policy for Drive Zip Extractor

Effective Date: October 8, 2026

1. Overview

Drive Zip Extractor ("the App") is an application designed to help users extract and inspect archive files directly within Google Drive. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting any information accessed during your use of the application.

2. Google User Data Accessed

The App requests access to your Google Drive account using Google OAuth. Specifically:

  • Accessed Files: The App only accesses the files you explicitly select or open to perform decompression/extraction operations.
  • No Full Drive Harvesting: The App does not index, scan, or harvest files unrelated to the extraction request.

3. How We Use User Data

  • Data read from your Google Drive is used solely in real time to decompress ZIP archives and save the extracted files back to your Drive.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or Google Drive files to third parties.
  • We do not retain copies of your files on external persistent storage.

4. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

Drive Zip Extractor's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

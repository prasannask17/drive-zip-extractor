Privacy Policy for Drive Zip Extractor

Effective Date: October 8, 2026
Last Updated: October 14, 2026

1. Overview

Drive Zip Extractor ("the App", "we", "us") is a web utility designed to allow users to inspect and extract compressed ZIP archives directly within their Google Drive storage. We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding any information accessed through our integration with Google APIs.

2. Google User Data Accessed

The App requests access to your Google Drive account using Google OAuth 2.0. Specifically:

  • Narrowest Scope (drive.file): The App uses the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. This grants access strictly to files you explicitly open or select with the App and permits creating new files and folders for the extracted content.
  • No General Drive Access: The App does not have permission to view, scan, index, modify, or delete any other files or folders in your Google Drive repository.

3. How We Use User Data

  • Real-Time Operation: Data read from your Google Drive is used exclusively in real time to unpack ZIP archives and deposit uncompressed files into your specified destination directory in Google Drive.
  • No Human Inspection: No developers, administrators, or contractors have access to view, read, or inspect your personal files or their extracted contents.
  • No Data Retention: File content is processed in transient volatile memory during the extraction session and is never persisted on external databases, disk storage, or third-party servers.
  • No Sale or Commercial Transfer: We do not sell, rent, license, or monetize your Google Drive data, personal information, or file contents.
  • No AI Model Training or Ads: Your Google Drive data is never used for targeted advertising, credit assessment, or training artificial intelligence and machine learning models.

4. Data Storage and Security

All data exchanges between your browser, the application, and Google servers occur exclusively over industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Session tokens issued by Google OAuth are maintained securely and are utilized solely to authenticate actions you trigger.

5. Sign-In Information

When authenticating through Google, the App receives standard authentication tokens and minimal profile details (such as basic email or account identifiers) solely to facilitate and identify the active session. This data is not harvested, sold, or shared.

6. Revoking Access and Deleting Data

You can revoke the App's permissions at any time through Google's official permissions manager: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Because Drive Zip Extractor stores no persistent copies of your files or personal records on external servers, there is no retained data to purge upon access revocation. Extracted files reside within your Google Drive account and remain under your complete control.

7. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

Drive Zip Extractor's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect modifications in functionality or legal requirements. When updates occur, the "Last Updated" timestamp at the top of this document will be revised accordingly. Continued usage of the application indicates acceptance of the updated terms.

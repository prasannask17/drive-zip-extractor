Privacy Policy for Drive Zip Extractor

Effective Date: October 8, 2026
Last Updated: October 14, 2026

1. Overview

Drive Zip Extractor ("the App") is an application designed to help users extract and inspect archive files directly within Google Drive. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting any information accessed during your use of the application.

2. Google User Data Accessed

The App requests access to your Google Drive account using Google OAuth. Specifically:

  • Accessed Files: The App uses the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope, so it only accesses the files you explicitly select or open with the App, and the files and folders the App creates, in order to perform decompression/extraction operations.
  • No Full Drive Harvesting: The App does not index, scan, or harvest files unrelated to the extraction request.

3. How We Use User Data

  • Data read from your Google Drive is used solely in real time to decompress ZIP archives and save the extracted files back to your Drive.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or Google Drive files to third parties.
  • We do not retain copies of your files on external persistent storage.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not use it to train any machine learning or AI models.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your files are processed temporarily in memory while an extraction is running and are not saved to any database or persistent storage by the App. Communication between the App and Google's services takes place over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Authorization tokens issued by Google are used only to carry out the actions you request.

5. Sign-In Information

When you sign in with Google, the App may receive basic account information (such as your name or email address) as part of the sign-in process. This information is used only to identify your session and is not stored, shared, or sold.

6. Revoking Access and Deleting Data

You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Because the App does not store your data, there is nothing further to delete on our side. Files the App extracted into your Google Drive belong to you and can be removed from Drive at any time. If you have any questions about your data, contact us at the email address below.

7. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

Drive Zip Extractor's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
